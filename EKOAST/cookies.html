Cookie Policy

Effective date: 1 February 2026 · Last reviewed: 22 November 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. They help a site remember things between visits, such as whether you are signed in or what is in your basket. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels; we refer to all of them as “cookies” in this policy.

2. What we use

We use strictly necessary storage to make the store work, and — only with your permission — optional analytics and personalisation storage. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

Category Purpose Consent
Strictly necessary Sign-in sessions, shopping basket, checkout, security and remembering your cookie choice. Always active
Analytics Aggregated information about pages visited and errors, used to improve the store. Optional — your choice
Personalisation & marketing Remembering preferences and showing relevant offers. Optional — your choice

3. Your choices

You can accept all cookies, reject all optional cookies, or choose category by category. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time using the button below or the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

Manage cookie settings

You can also control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent sign-in, basket and checkout from working correctly.

4. Retention

Session storage is removed when you close your browser. Your cookie preference is kept for up to 12 months. Analytics data is retained only for as long as needed for the purpose described above, then deleted or aggregated.

5. Third parties

Our store is hosted on Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure and uses Google signs-in where you choose that option. Payment details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers. Third-party providers may process data under their own terms. Fonts, icons and product imagery are served from our own domain, not third-party CDNs.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our practices change and revise the date above. Questions can be sent to support@ekoast.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
