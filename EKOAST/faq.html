Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping on .

Orders & Delivery

How do I track my order?
You can track your order status by visiting the 'Track Order' page and entering your order ID. Alternatively, login to your account and view your order history.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery timelines vary by location. Generally, orders within Lagos take 1-3 business days, while orders to other states take 3-7 business days.
Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
If your order hasn't been shipped yet, please contact our support team immediately to update your address. Once shipped, addresses cannot be changed.

Payments & Refunds

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major Debit/Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), Bank Transfers, and USSD payments via our secure payment gateway.
Is it safe to use my credit card on ?
Yes, absolutely. All payments are processed through PCIDSS-compliant payment processors. We do not store your full card details on our servers.
How do I get a refund?
If you return an eligible item, we will process your refund to your original payment method or Wallet within 5-7 business days of receiving the return.

Account & Services

How do I reset my password?
Go to the Login page and click on "Forgot Password?". Enter your email address, and we will send you a link to reset it.
Do you offer warranty on products?
Yes, most electronic items come with a manufacturer's warranty. The specific warranty period is listed on the product page.
