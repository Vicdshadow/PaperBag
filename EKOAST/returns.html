Returns & Refunds Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

Return Timeline

You have 7 days from the date of delivery to return an item. Items must be in their original condition, unused, and with all tags and packaging intact.

How to Return an Item

1
Initiate Return: Login to your account, go to 'Orders', and select the item you wish to return.
2
Pack Item: Place the item in its original packaging, including all accessories and free gifts.
3
Drop-off or Pickup: We will arrange for a retrieval of the item or provide a drop-off location near you.

Eligibility for Returns

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Perishable goods (e.g., food)
  • Personal hygiene items
  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Software or digital downloads once accessed

Refund Process

Once we receive your returned item, we will inspect it. If approved, your refund will be processed to your original method of payment or Wallet within 5-7 business days.

Warranty Claims

For electronics and appliances, manufacturer warranties apply. Please refer to the warranty card included in your package or contact support for assistance.
