Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: 22 November 2026

Our commitment

PAPERBAG wants everyone to be able to browse and shop with confidence. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA and the U.S. Section 508 baseline, and to respect the European Accessibility Act where it applies to our services.

What we have done

  • Keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images, and labels for icon-only controls.
  • Sufficient colour contrast, scalable text, and layouts that reflow on small screens.
  • Forms with programmatic labels and accessible error messages where available.
  • Animations and transitions that are reduced automatically when your device requests reduced motion.
  • Semantic headings, landmarks and language declarations on every page.

Known limitations

Some older admin and catalogue pages contain legacy inline styles and data tables that may not yet be fully accessible at every viewport size. Product videos, where present, may not yet include captions. We are working through these areas as part of ongoing improvements.

Feedback and help

If you encounter a barrier, please email support@ekoast.com with the page URL and a description of the problem. We aim to respond within 5 working days and will provide information in an alternative format where possible.

Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the relevant national enforcement body in your country (for example, the Equality and Human Rights Commission in the UK or a national market surveillance authority in the EU).
