Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 February 2026 · Last reviewed: 22 November 2026

1. Who we are

PAPERBAG (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates this marketplace and is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at support@ekoast.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number and password hash when you register.
  • Order and delivery information: billing and delivery addresses, items ordered, delivery instructions and order history.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment provider. We receive transaction status and limited payment metadata; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support chat, emails and any reviews you choose to publish.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and error logs, collected through necessary storage and, with your consent, analytics.
  • Seller information: if you sell on PAPERBAG, business details, bank/payout details and tax information required to pay you and meet legal obligations.

3. Why we use your information

Purpose Legal basis
Create and manage your account, process and deliver ordersPerformance of a contract
Take payment, prevent fraud, keep records for tax and accountingContract and legal obligation
Answer support requests and send service messagesContract and legitimate interests
Improve the store, measure performance and understand usageYour consent (analytics cookies)
Send offers or personalised contentYour consent (marketing cookies or opt-in)

We do not use your personal data for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Who we share with

  • Sellers on PAPERBAG: the seller of an item receives the information needed to fulfil your order (such as name and delivery address).
  • Service providers: hosting and database services (Firebase / Google Cloud), payment processors, delivery and logistics partners, email and support tools. They act under contract and may only use data for the services they provide to us.
  • Authorities: where disclosure is required by law or to protect rights, safety and fraud prevention.
  • Corporate transactions: in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the country where you live. Where that happens, we rely on applicable legal safeguards such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms, together with additional technical and organisational measures.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you close your account, then removed within 30 days except where law requires retention.
  • Order, invoice and tax records: up to 6 years after the transaction, as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Support conversations: up to 24 months after the matter is resolved.
  • Cookie preference: up to 12 months; analytics data only as long as needed for the stated purpose.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you and receive it in a portable format.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Ask us to delete your data or restrict its use.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including analytics or marketing cookies, without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it for advertising).
  • Complain to your local data protection or privacy regulator.

To exercise a right, email support@ekoast.com. We will verify your identity and respond within the time limit required by applicable law (normally one month). You may also change optional cookie choices at any time via the cookie settings page.

8. Children

Our store is intended for people aged 18 or over, or younger users shopping with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, least-privilege credentials and monitoring to protect personal data. No system is completely secure; if a breach occurs we will notify you and regulators where the law requires.

10. Cookies

We use strictly necessary storage to operate the store and optional analytics and personalisation storage only with your consent. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The date at the top shows when it was last reviewed. Questions: support@ekoast.com. See also our Terms & Conditions and Accessibility Statement.
